History of Trisatva Constructions Private Limited

A New Beginning (2026)

Trisatva Constructions Private Limited was incorporated in 2026 with a vision to become a leading construction company in Bihar. Founded by a team of experienced professionals from the construction and engineering sectors, the company was established with a commitment to quality, innovation, and excellence in infrastructure development.

Though newly established, Trisatva brings together decades of combined experience from its founders and team members who have worked on major construction projects across Bihar and other states. The company was created to address the growing infrastructure needs of Bihar with modern construction practices and professional project management.

Vision and Foundation

Trisatva Constructions was founded on the principles of delivering high-quality construction services, maintaining ethical business practices, and contributing to Bihar's infrastructure development. The company's name "Trisatva" reflects the three essential qualities (Tri-Satva) that guide our operations:

  • Quality (Sattva): Unwavering commitment to construction excellence
  • Innovation (Rajas): Embracing modern technologies and creative solutions
  • Stability (Tamas): Building lasting and durable infrastructure

Company Incorporation - 2026

The company was officially incorporated in February 2026 as a Private Limited Company under the Companies Act, 2013. The incorporation marked the beginning of our journey to establish Trisatva as a trusted name in Bihar's construction industry.

Key Details of Incorporation:

  • Company Name: Trisatva Constructions Private Limited
  • Year of Incorporation: 2026
  • Registered Office: Patna, Bihar
  • Business Activity: Construction and Infrastructure Development
  • Service Area: Bihar and neighboring states

Founding Team

Trisatva Constructions was founded by a team of professionals with extensive experience in construction, engineering, and project management:

Board of Directors:
Our directors bring over 50 years of combined experience in the construction industry, having worked on major infrastructure projects, residential complexes, and commercial developments across India.

Technical Team:
Our technical team comprises qualified civil engineers, structural engineers, and construction managers with proven track records in successfully delivering complex construction projects.

Our Mission from Day One

From its inception, Trisatva Constructions has been committed to:

  • Delivering construction projects that meet the highest quality standards
  • Using modern construction technologies and sustainable practices
  • Providing employment opportunities to local workforce
  • Contributing to Bihar's economic development through infrastructure projects
  • Building long-term relationships with clients based on trust and transparency
  • Maintaining the highest standards of safety on all construction sites

Initial Focus Areas

As a new company, Trisatva Constructions has identified the following focus areas:

Residential Construction

Apartment complexes, housing societies, and individual homes with modern amenities and quality construction.

Commercial Projects

Office buildings, shopping complexes, and commercial spaces for businesses and enterprises.

Infrastructure Development

Roads, bridges, and urban infrastructure projects contributing to Bihar's growth.

Building for the Future

While Trisatva Constructions is a new entrant in Bihar's construction sector, we are committed to building a legacy of excellence. Our goal is not just to construct buildings and infrastructure, but to create lasting value for our clients, employees, and the communities we serve.
