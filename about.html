About Trisatva Constructions Private Limited

Company Overview

Trisatva Constructions Private Limited is a newly incorporated construction company established in 2026, headquartered in Patna, Bihar. We are committed to delivering excellence in construction and infrastructure development across Bihar and beyond.

Our company brings together a team of experienced professionals from the construction, engineering, and project management sectors. We combine modern construction techniques with traditional values of quality, integrity, and customer satisfaction.

Our Vision

To become a leading construction company in Bihar, recognized for delivering high-quality infrastructure projects that contribute to the state's development and improve the quality of life for its citizens.

Our Mission

  • Deliver construction projects with the highest standards of quality and safety
  • Provide innovative and cost-effective solutions to our clients
  • Maintain transparency and ethical practices in all our operations
  • Contribute to Bihar's infrastructure development and economic growth
  • Create employment opportunities and support local communities
  • Adopt sustainable and environmentally responsible construction practices

Our Expertise

Trisatva Constructions specializes in a wide range of construction services:

  • Residential Construction: Apartments, housing complexes, and individual homes
  • Commercial Buildings: Office complexes, shopping centers, and commercial spaces
  • Infrastructure Projects: Roads, highways, bridges, and drainage systems
  • Industrial Construction: Warehouses, factories, and industrial facilities
  • Government Projects: Public buildings, schools, hospitals, and civic infrastructure
  • Project Management: End-to-end project planning, execution, and delivery
